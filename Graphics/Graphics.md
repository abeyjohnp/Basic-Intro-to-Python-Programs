GRAPHICS
Turtle will move through the window.
Tieing a pen to tail of a turtle, wherever it moves, it keeps drawing.
* Cordinate system is cartesian system.
- Attributes
* Heading
* Colour
* Width
* Down 
# Turtle Methods
1. t= Turtle()
2. t.home()
Trying to retrive the home position
3. t.up()
Move pen up
4. t.down()
5. t.setheading(degree)
Specify direction
- east : 0
- west : 180
- south : 270
- north : 90

6. t.fillcolor(r,g,b)
7. t.begin_fill()
8. t.end_fill()
9. t.clear()
10. t.widht(pixels)
11. t.hideturtle()
12. t.showturtle()
13. t.position()
14. t.heading()
15. t.isdown()
16. t.screen.bgcolor("color") - give colour to window
17. t.shape("") - turtle shape can be made to circle, square
18. t.speed(0) - 0 is fast - no animation, 10 is fasted with animation, 1-9 (slow-fast)
